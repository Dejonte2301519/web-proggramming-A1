LLMs as a Tool, Not a Crutch

(Joseph J. Slade, 2025) states that AI is a powerful tool that can assist in learning and problem-solving, but it should not replace learning. When using LLMs, it is important to critically evaluate the information provided and cross-check it with reliable sources. LLMs can be used to generate ideas, provide explanations, and offer different perspectives, but they should not be relied upon as the sole source of information. It is essential to use LLMs responsibly and ensure that they are used to enhance learning rather than replace it.
